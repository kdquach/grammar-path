## 1. Pedagogical Scope & Invariant Constraints

This specification defines the 8 distinct question blueprints for the Unit 3 TOEIC Part 5 pilot generation.

### 1.1 Scope Boundaries
- **Target Unit:** Murphy Unit 3 (*Present continuous and present simple 1*).
- **Core Grammatical Concepts Tested:**
  1. Present continuous for temporary actions and situations taking place around now (Section 3B).
  2. Present continuous affirmative, negative, and interrogative syntactical forms.
  3. Aspectual contrast: Present continuous (temporary situation) versus Present simple (routine/habitual baseline) (Section 3A vs 3B).
- **Mandatory Exclusions & Negative Constraints:**
  1. **STRICT EXCLUSION OF SECTION 3C:** Raymond Murphy's Section 3C (*"I always do and I'm always doing"* / complaint idioms like *"He is always losing his keys"*) is **STRICTLY EXCLUDED** from this pilot. It represents an informal colloquial register unsuitable for ETS TOEIC Part 5 standardized corporate assessments.
  2. **STRICT EXCLUSION OF UNIT 4 STATIVE VERBS:** Absolutely NO stative verbs from Unit 4 (*know, believe, understand, like, want, need, belong, remember, prefer, contain*). Any occurrence triggers immediate Stage 3 / Stage 7 rejection.
  3. **ZERO TEXTBOOK COPYING:** All protagonist names, organizational names, sentence structures, and lexical items are 100% newly authored. Zero sentences are derived from Cambridge University Press exercises or examples.

### Blueprint U03-P01: Temporary Corporate Relocation
- **Blueprint ID:** `U03-P01-AFF-FACILITIES`
- **Grammatical Objective:** Test singular present continuous affirmative (*is operating*) to describe a temporary operational arrangement resulting from ongoing facility renovations.
- **Workplace Domain:** Facilities Management & Corporate Real Estate.
- **Protagonist:** The regional customer support department.
- **Target Verb:** `operate` (Singular auxiliary concord: *is operating*).
- **Mandatory Carrier Skeleton:**  
  `Due to unexpected electrical maintenance in the primary building, the customer service division _______ from a temporary satellite facility throughout this week.`
- **Option Architecture:**
  - **(A) operates:** Distractor — Simple present; fails to express the temporary, abnormal condition defined by *"throughout this week"*.
  - **(B) is operating:** **CORRECT KEY** — Present continuous affirmative; perfectly matches temporary action in progress.
  - **(C) operated:** Distractor — Past simple; contradicts the ongoing current timeframe (*"throughout this week"*).
  - **(D) operating:** Distractor — Bare present participle; lacks mandatory finite auxiliary verb *is*.
- **Clue Anchor:** *"throughout this week"* + *"temporary satellite facility"*.

### Blueprint U03-P02: IT Cloud Migration Project
- **Blueprint ID:** `U03-P02-AFF-INFRASTRUCTURE`
- **Grammatical Objective:** Test plural present continuous affirmative (*are transferring*) to describe an ongoing multi-day data migration task.
- **Workplace Domain:** Information Technology & Data Security.
- **Protagonist:** The database engineering team (plural collective / plural noun phrase).
- **Target Verb:** `transfer` (Plural auxiliary concord: *are transferring*).
- **Mandatory Carrier Skeleton:**  
  `Network administrators _______ sensitive financial records to the encrypted secondary server right now to prevent data loss during the scheduled outage.`
- **Option Architecture:**
  - **(A) are transferring:** **CORRECT KEY** — Plural present continuous; correctly pairs auxiliary *are* with participle *transferring* for action occurring *"right now"*.
  - **(B) transfer:** Distractor — Base/simple present; suggests permanent routine rather than active intervention *"right now"*.
  - **(C) transferred:** Distractor — Past simple; contradicts immediate temporal anchor.
  - **(D) have transferred:** Distractor — Present perfect; implies the migration is already finished rather than currently underway.
- **Clue Anchor:** *"right now to prevent data loss"*.

- **Mandatory Carrier Skeleton:**  
  `Because technical staff are performing emergency database maintenance tonight, the international payment portal _______ credit transactions at this time.`
- **Option Architecture:**
  - **(A) does not process:** Distractor — Simple present negative; incorrectly signifies a permanent business policy of never accepting credit cards.
  - **(B) is not processing:** **CORRECT KEY** — Present continuous negative; precisely denotes temporary non-operation at the current time.
  - **(C) not processing:** Distractor — Incomplete negative verbal; omits required finite auxiliary *is*.
  - **(D) was not processed:** Distractor — Past passive; introduces incorrect voice and past timeframe.
- **Clue Anchor:** *"tonight"* + *"at this time"*.

### Blueprint U03-P05: Regulatory Facility Audit
- **Blueprint ID:** `U03-P05-INT-COMPLIANCE`
- **Grammatical Objective:** Test present participle verb form (*inspecting*) in inverted interrogative syntax (*"Is the lead auditor _______...?"*).
- **Workplace Domain:** Quality Assurance & Environmental Regulatory Compliance.
- **Protagonist:** The lead compliance inspector.
- **Target Verb:** `inspect` (Participle form in inverted question: *inspecting*).
- **Mandatory Carrier Skeleton:**  
  `Is the municipal environmental inspector _______ the manufacturing plant right now, or did the management team postpone the walk-through?`
- **Option Architecture:**
  - **(A) inspects:** Distractor — Third-person singular; cannot follow finite auxiliary *Is* in continuous question syntax.
  - **(B) inspect:** Distractor — Bare base form; requires auxiliary *Does* rather than *Is*.
  - **(C) inspecting:** **CORRECT KEY** — Present participle; completes the inverted continuous construction initiated by *Is*.
  - **(D) inspected:** Distractor — Past participle; forms a passive question (*"Is the inspector inspected?"*), which creates an absurd semantic defect.
- **Clue Anchor:** Initial inverted auxiliary *"Is"* + *"right now"*.

### Blueprint U03-P07: Routine Rail vs. Temporary Air Freight (3A vs 3B Contrast)
- **Blueprint ID:** `U03-P07-CON-LOGISTICS`
- **Grammatical Objective:** Test the contrast between routine scheduled action (present simple: *usually transports*) and temporary operational exception (present continuous: *is dispatching*).
- **Workplace Domain:** Global Supply Chain & Distribution Logistics.
- **Protagonist:** The central distribution center.
- **Target Verb:** `dispatch` (Contrastive continuous key: *is dispatching*).
- **Mandatory Carrier Skeleton:**  
  `The logistics center usually transports industrial machinery by cargo train, but this week it _______ urgent shipments via air courier to prevent assembly line delays.`
- **Option Architecture:**
  - **(A) dispatches:** Distractor — Simple present; ignores the explicit temporary contrast introduced by *"but this week"*.
  - **(B) is dispatching:** **CORRECT KEY** — Present continuous; conveys the temporary deviation from standard transportation protocol.
  - **(C) dispatched:** Distractor — Past simple; fails to represent the active ongoing temporary response.
  - **(D) dispatch:** Distractor — Base form / agreement error with third-person singular pronoun *it*.
- **Clue Anchor:** Coordinate contrast *"usually transports... but this week it _______"*.

### Blueprint U03-P08: Standard Hiring vs. Temporary Overseas Recruitment (3A vs 3B Contrast)
- **Blueprint ID:** `U03-P08-CON-HR`
- **Grammatical Objective:** Test the contrast between established organizational habit (present simple: *typically hires*) and current temporary staffing initiative (present continuous: *is recruiting*).
- **Workplace Domain:** Human Resources & International Talent Acquisition.
- **Protagonist:** Apex Technologies software division.
- **Target Verb:** `recruit` (Contrastive continuous key: *is recruiting*).
- **Mandatory Carrier Skeleton:**  
  `Apex Technologies typically hires local engineering graduates, but currently the human resources team _______ specialized software architects from abroad for the autonomous vehicle project.`
- **Option Architecture:**
  - **(A) is recruiting:** **CORRECT KEY** — Present continuous; captures the temporary, focused hiring campaign indicated by *"currently"*.
  - **(B) recruits:** Distractor — Simple present; obliterates the contrast with *"typically hires"*.
  - **(C) recruited:** Distractor — Past simple; contradicts the active adverbial *"currently"*.
  - **(D) recruit:** Distractor — Agreement error; collective singular subject *"team"* requires singular agreement.
- **Clue Anchor:** Coordinate contrast *"typically hires... but currently the team _______"*.
